NeurIPS 2025

Memory-Efficient Visual Autoregressive Modeling with Scale-Aware KV Cache

Kunjun Li, Zigeng Chen, Cheng-Yen Yang, Jenq-Neng Hwang

NeurIPS 2025

Scale-aware cache management reduces KV memory by 90% without quality loss, making visual autoregressive generation faster and practical at resolutions up to 4K.

Paper Code
Abstract
Visual Autoregressive modeling offers efficient, scalable next-scale prediction, but its coarse-to-fine process produces a rapidly growing KV cache. ScaleKV identifies scale-specific drafters and refiners from their distinct attention patterns, then gives each group differentiated cache capacity. On the Infinity model family, it preserves pixel-level fidelity while reducing KV-cache memory to 10% of the original.
CVPR 2025

TinyFusion: Diffusion Transformers Learned Shallow

Gongfan Fang*, Kunjun Li*, Xinyin Ma, Xinchao Wang (co-first author)

CVPR 2025 Highlight (Top 3%)

An end-to-end learnable depth-pruning framework that halves Diffusion Transformer depth and parameters, delivering 2× faster inference at only 7% of the original training cost.

Paper Code
Abstract
TinyFusion learns which Diffusion Transformer layers to remove by optimizing a pruned model for recoverability after fine-tuning. Differentiable sampling and a co-optimized parameter simulate future fine-tuning during pruning, outperforming importance- and error-based alternatives across DiTs, MARs, and SiTs.
IPSN 2024

PixelGen: Rethinking Embedded Camera Systems for Mixed-Reality

Kunjun Li, Manoj Gulati, Dhairya Shah, Steven Waskito, Shantanu Chakrabarty, Ambuj Varshney

ACM/IEEE IPSN 2024 Best Demo Runner-Up

A low-power camera system that combines monochrome images with sensor signals and diffusion models to visualize information beyond the visible spectrum.

Paper Code
Abstract
PixelGen rethinks mixed-reality camera systems by combining energy-efficient monochrome imagery and low-bandwidth sensors with diffusion-based image generation. The result is a richer representation of the environment that can reveal otherwise invisible physical phenomena.
